02 About

I'm a 3D designer based in Cairo, working with brands, furniture makers and studios who need their products rendered before a single unit is manufactured.

Over the past three years I've moved from generalist 3D work into a focused practice: product visualization, furniture design and architectural interiors. Most projects start the same way — a rough brief, a few reference photos, sometimes just a sketch — and end with a set of renders a client can put directly into a campaign, a catalog or a pitch deck. I care about the process staying practical: clear turnarounds, real feedback rounds, files that are actually usable downstream.
